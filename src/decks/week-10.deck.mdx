## Descendants

Languages that keep Metamath's small verifier and change something else.

- Metamath Zero
- Ghilbert and JHilbert
- Russell

Then the translators, in and out. Then one comparison.

```notes
The thing they keep is the property the whole course rests on: a verifier
small enough to be written by one person. What each changes is the interest.
```

## Metamath Zero

"a language for writing specifications and proofs", inspired by "Metamath and
Lean, two proof languages at opposite ends of a spectrum."

- Separates specification from proof
- Adds Metamath One, in which proofs are written and compiled

[digama0/mm0](https://github.com/digama0/mm0)

```notes
The repository's own description. Wednesday you read its hand-translation of
set.mm's axioms beside set.mm itself.
```

## Ghilbert and JHilbert

Raph Levien's language, "that has its roots in Metamath but guarantees the
soundness of definitions" — Megill, in Wiedijk's survey
([PDF, p. 104](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~freek/comparison/comparison.pdf)).

The Metamath site lists its verifier gh_verify, and JHilbert, "written in Java
by Alexander Klauer" ([other.html](https://us.metamath.org/other.html)).

```notes
Soundness of definitions is what week 7's definition exercise was about.
Ghilbert put it in the language.
```

## Russell

Its mdl program is "a compiler from a relativily high-level language for the
representation of formal mathematics to the simple and robust for checking
language Metamath."

[dmitry-vlasov/russell](https://github.com/dmitry-vlasov/russell) · download
dated 10-Apr-2013 on [other.html](https://us.metamath.org/other.html)

```notes
The quotation is exact, spelling included. The idea: write at a higher level
and compile down to Metamath, so the thing checked stays simple.
```

## Translations in

Carneiro's 2014 paper, as [its arXiv record](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.1412.8091)
describes it, converts proofs from OpenTheory, an exchange format for "the HOL
family of proof languages", into Metamath: first into hol.mm, then into set.mm.

```notes
This is why week 8 flagged hol.mm. It is the landing point: a HOL proof
arrives there first, and is carried into set.mm second.
```

## Translations out, and in again

- `mm0-hs from-mm`: Metamath to MM0
  ([digama0/mm0](https://github.com/digama0/mm0))
- [mm2dk](https://gitlab.com/semantiko/mm2dk/translator): Metamath to Dedukti
- [AML-Lean-to-Metamath](https://gitlab.com/ilds/aml-lean/AML-Lean-to-Metamath)
  and [kore2mm](https://github.com/zhengyao-lin/kore2mm): matching logic into
  Metamath, from Lean and from Kore

```notes
Two directions. Out of Metamath into a descendant and into Dedukti; into
Metamath from two matching-logic projects. A format this small is easy to
target.
```

## One proof, seventeen systems

[The Seventeen Provers of the World](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~freek/comparison/comparison.pdf):
the irrationality of the square root of 2, in seventeen systems.

Metamath's entry, pp. 103–105, is by Megill.

```notes
Same theorem in every system, so the proofs can be read side by side. The
Metamath pages are the second item on the reading list.
```

## Five neighbours, in their own words

- [Lean](https://lean-lang.org/): "an open-source programming language and
  proof assistant"
- [Rocq Prover](https://rocq-prover.org/): "formerly known as the Coq Proof
  Assistant"
- [Isabelle](http://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/Research/HVG/Isabelle/index.html): "a
  generic proof assistant"
- [HOL Light](http://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~jrh13/hol-light/): "higher order logic"
- Mizar: "whereas Metamath shows you every detail"
  ([Metamath home page](https://us.metamath.org/index.html))

```notes
Four descriptions from the projects' own front pages, and one from Metamath's
home page, which says Mizar mimics published proofs. Names to search, not a
ranking.
```
