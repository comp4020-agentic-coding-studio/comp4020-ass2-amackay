The last lecture collects the pieces that fit under no earlier week.

**Metamath Solitaire** is a Java applet, downloadable from the
[home page](https://us.metamath.org/index.html) as
[mmsolitaire.zip](https://us.metamath.org/downloads/mmsolitaire.zip). In
Wiedijk's survey Megill says "a unification algorithm lets the user know exactly
what possibilities are available at any point in a proof", and that it "is not
practical for larger proofs"
([PDF, p. 104](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~freek/comparison/comparison.pdf)).

**The shortest Principia proofs.**
[pmproofs.txt](https://us.metamath.org/mmsolitaire/pmproofs.txt) holds proofs
of "all 193 theorems of propositional calculus" in Principia Mathematica from
ax-1, ax-2, ax-3 and ax-mp, in a notation the file calls condensed detachment.
Its revision history runs from Gregory Bush's 2004 shortening of 67 proofs to
Samiro Discher's 2025 shortening of 38 with pmGenerator.

**Open problems.** The
[workshop miscellany page](https://us.metamath.org/award2003.html) "was
originally created as part of a presentation at the 2003 Argonne Workshop on
Automated Reasoning and Deduction"; "Items 2 through 17 each present one or
more open problems (which in some cases have been solved, as noted)". Item 9
asks whether Metamath's predicate-calculus axioms are independent.

**Where people talk.** "The Metamath Google Group mailing list is a great place
to discuss Metamath" ([home page](https://us.metamath.org/index.html);
[the group](https://groups.google.com/g/metamath)). The site's
[other page](https://us.metamath.org/other.html) also lists a Gitter chat room
"set up by Mario Carneiro".

**Meetings.** Megill's
[Institut Henri Poincaré slides](https://us.metamath.org/downloads/ihp2014mm.pdf)
of 9 May 2014 open with "Very small verifier (≈300 lines code)". The other page
records Big Proof 2017, where "Mario Carneiro presents Metamath in the first
part of this talk", and
[FOMM 2020](http://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/avigad/meetings/fomm2020/index.html),
held at Carnegie Mellon from 6 to 10 January 2020, where Carneiro's talk was
"Metamath Zero, or: How to Verify a Verifier"
([other.html](https://us.metamath.org/other.html)).

**The record, drawn.** "Metamath Proof Explorer (set.mm) contributions
visualized with Gource through 2019-10-04" is
[a video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XC1g8FmFcUU) on the same page.

**The organisation.** [github.com/metamath](https://github.com/metamath) was
created on 11 September 2013 and holds, among others,
[metamath-exe](https://github.com/metamath/metamath-exe), "source code for the
Metamath executable", and [set.mm](https://github.com/metamath/set.mm).

**History.** The
[set.mm README](https://github.com/metamath/set.mm/blob/develop/README.md)
dedicates the collection to the memory of Norman "Norm" Dwight Megill, Ph.D.
(1950-2021), "creator of the Metamath system and cultivator of an international
community of people with the shared dream of digitizing and formally verifying
mathematics".
