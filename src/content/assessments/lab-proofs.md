## The brief

> Prove the theorem the lab sets, and hand in the proof.

Every lab from week 3 to week 11 ends with a theorem to prove in mmj2, usually
started in the room and finished afterwards. The theorem is chosen so that the
week's lecture is what you need to prove it, and so that the proof takes an
hour, not an evening. The best eight of the nine count, so one bad week costs
nothing.

## What you submit

The proof file the lab names, by 5pm on the Friday of that week, through the
course submission page. Nothing else: no write-up, no screenshot.

## How it is marked

A proof that the verifier accepts gets the first 80% of the marks for that
week. The remaining 20% is for a proof a person can read: steps in an order
that follows the argument, no unused steps, and hypotheses named as the lab
asked. A proof that does not verify gets no marks that week, whatever else it
has; that is the whole point of the subject, and it is also why the best
eight count rather than all nine.
